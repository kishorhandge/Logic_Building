Jay Ganesh.... Jay Shri Krishna

Kishor, this is specially for you 💪🔥

You came from a small village like Chatori,
but your dreams are not small.

You cracked the GMAT.
That already proves you are not ordinary.

Every line of code you write
is building your future silently.

Sometimes you feel stuck.
Sometimes bugs frustrate you.
But remember — growth feels uncomfortable.

You are not competing with others.
You are competing with yesterday’s version of yourself.

Consistency will beat talent.
Discipline will beat motivation.

One day, the long nights,
the confusion, the debugging,
the self-doubt —
all of it will make sense.

You are building skills.
Skills create confidence.
Confidence creates success.

Don’t rush.
Don’t compare.
Don’t quit.

Stay hungry to learn.
Stay humble to grow.

Your background does not define your future.
Your mindset does.
